The largest rPET platter range from Sabert

Sabert offers the largest assortment of recycled and recyclable PET platters, trays and lids, with a very large choice of design, colours and dimensions. Thanks to an exclusive lamination process, Sabert's black, marble and silver platters offer outstanding renders with the perfect illusion of stainless steel — becoming some of the best sandwich trays with a lid option on the market.

An adapted lid is available for each platter tray, with an efficient closing system and strong stackability, guaranteeing secure food transportation from kitchen to counter or event.

  • Ultra-secure clip-on lid systems help save time during service
  • High quality lamination delivers a genuinely life-like result
  • Stays rigid with liquid, wet and oily cold food
  • Cardboard catering boxes also available for our main platter solutions
